How To Cast
To This Screen

Cast your phone, tablet, or laptop to enjoy content on the big screen instantly.

🖥️
Device name on this TV

DG office





Tips Before You Cast

For the best casting experience

📶

Same Wi-Fi Network

Make sure your device and TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

⚡

Performance May Vary

Streaming quality depends on network speed, device performance, and signal strength.

🔄

Switching Devices

Wait a few seconds after disconnecting before another device starts casting.

📍

Stay Close To The TV

Keep your device relatively close to the TV and Wi-Fi router for stability.





Steps To Cast

Step 1

Turn On The TV

Press the power button on your remote to turn on the TV.

Step 2

Select Cast On The TV

Open the Connection Tips screen and ensure the Cast option is highlighted.

Step 3

Open Cast / Screen Mirror

Android

Open Quick Settings and tap Cast or Smart View.

Windows

Press Windows + K and choose Connect.

iPhone / iPad

Open Control Center and tap Screen Mirroring.

Step 4

Choose Your TV

From the list of available devices, select DG office.

Step 5

Start Casting

Your device screen will now appear on the TV. Enjoy your content on the big screen.
